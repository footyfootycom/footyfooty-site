PART 1 · THE PEAKS

Up where the gondolas hum

A cable line of little red cars strung between cliff-top cabins, far above the mist.

PART 2 · THE FALLS

Stone frogs guard the tunnels

Waterfalls pour out of carved frog-face arches. The river ducks through and keeps going.

PART 3 · THE LANES

Boardwalks zig. Rivers zag.

Lookout towers, arch bridges and turquoise lanes that split, loop and meet again.

PART 4 · THE SPIRES

Rope bridges and rock towers

Below the big bend the river braids around sandstone spires, ducks into caves and tumbles down the stepped rapids past the old water-wheel mill.

PART 5 · THE CANYON

Over the edge we go

The whole river pours off the cliff into a red-rock gorge. Mine carts rattle along the wall and the river keeps on running.
